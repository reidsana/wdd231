RiverLine Fly Fishing

Individual Project Site Plan

Site Name

RiverLine Fly Fishing

This name was selected because it reflects the flowing nature of rivers and the guiding experience offered by the service. It is simple, memorable, and clearly associated with fly fishing.

Optional domain availability: riverlineflyfishing.com

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to provide information about a fly fishing guide service, including guided trip options, seasonal fishing conditions, recommended gear, and booking details. The site will serve as an informational hub for anglers planning a guided fly fishing experience.

Scenarios

  • What guided fly fishing trips are available during different seasons?
  • What gear should I bring on a guided fly fishing trip?
  • How can I contact or book a guided trip?

Color Scheme

  • Forest Green (#1f4d3a): Used for headers, navigation areas, and footer backgrounds.
  • Golden Amber (#d9a441): Used for accents, borders, and highlights.
  • Light Gray (#f4f6f5): Used as the main background color.

This color scheme reflects nature, rivers, and outdoor environments while maintaining good contrast and accessibility.

Typography

  • Montserrat: Used for headings and section titles.
  • Roboto: Used for body text and paragraphs.

These fonts were chosen for readability, modern appearance, and strong accessibility support.
